About Outer Wilds

Outer Wilds is a space exploration game developed by Mobius Digital. The game follows the story of an astronaut who awakens to find their ship has been destroyed and they are stranded on a mysterious planet.

Gameplay

The gameplay of Outer Wilds is entirely driven by the players own curiosity and knowledge. There are no traditional menus or loading screens, no traditional upgrades, leveling systems, or unlockable weapons instead the player must rely on their observations and memory to progress through the game.

Outer Wilds Soundtrack

If there is one song that perfectly captures the cozy yet lonely feeling of space travel, its the haunting but beautiful melody of Outer Wilds theme song. Composed by Andrew Prahlow, it starts out with a simple, acoustic banjo melody around a campfire and builds into a powerful/massive space anthem.
