Welcome to my favourite video games website!

Video games have always been one of my favourite forms of entertainment. What I enjoy the most about them is how they can create memorable characters, unique worlds, and expereiences that are difficult to find anywhere else. Over the years I've played many different games, but there are a few that have stood out to me more than others.

This websitre explores three of my favourite games and franchises: Kingdom Hearts, The legend of Zelda, and Outer Wilds. Each game offers a very different experience, from exploring mysterious worlds and uncovering hidden stories to solving puzzles and going on epic adventures.

Why these games?

The games featured on this website are very different from one another, but they all share qualities that I really enjoy in video games. They each create worlds that encourage the player to explore, discover new things, and become invested in the experience.

Whether it is following and emotional story, exploring a vast world, or uncovering a mystery, these games have each given me memorable experiences that have stayed with me long after playing them.
