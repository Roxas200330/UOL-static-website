About Kingdom Hearts

Kingdom Hearts is an action role-playing game series developed by Square Enix and Disney Interactive. The series is known for its unique blend of characters and worlds from various Disney franchises and the Final Fantasy video game series.

Gameplay

The gameplay of Kingdom Hearts combines elements of action RPGs with the unique ability to switch between different worlds, each with their own distinct environments and challenges. Players control Sora, a young boy who wields a keyblade, as he journeys through various Disney and Final Fantasy worlds to save them from darkness. Kingdom Hearts Theme song

Nothing hits you in the feels more than the iconic theme song of Kingdom Hearts "Dearly Beloved". It plays on the main menu of every game but different renditions and the second those soft piano notes and ocean waves start up, you get hit with a wave of nostalgia and emotion. Yoko Shimomura absolutley nailed it. Its super beautiful but also melancholic/sad which captures perfectly what Kingdom hearts is about: wandering through worlds, missing your friends and holding onto hope.
